## About me

I'm an engineering student in **Data Science** at **Télécom Paris**, in a double degree with **INPT** (Rabat), with a minor in **Markets, Organizations, Data & Strategy**.

I like the whole journey of a data project: understanding the real question, wrangling the data, building the model, and **shipping something people actually use**. Lately, that has meant estimating poverty for an entire country, helping doctors search medical knowledge with AI, and orchestrating teams of AI agents.

My long-term goal: become an **AI architect** who speaks both *tech* and *strategy*.
