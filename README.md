## About me

I'm an engineering student in **Data Science** at **Télécom Paris**, in a double degree with **INPT** (Rabat), with a minor in **Markets, Organizations, Data & Strategy**.

I enjoy the whole journey of a data project: understanding the real question, building reliable data pipelines, designing models that hold up outside the notebook, and **shipping tools people actually use**. My long-term goal is to become an **AI architect** who speaks both tech and strategy.
